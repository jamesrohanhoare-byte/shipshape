# Sketch 001: Tasks Day Board

## Design Question
The combined Tasks model = BlitzBooks' day-strip/time engine + ShipShape's crew/status workflow.
This sketch tests the one open layout question: within a selected day, do you **filter to one
status at a time** (calm, focused) or **see the whole day's board** (To do / Doing / Done stacked)?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/001-tasks-day-board/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Filtered** — day strip + segmented To do / Doing / Done; one status list visible at a time. Closest to current ShipShape.
- **B: Whole-day board** — day strip + all three status groups stacked on one scroll. Full picture, more scrolling.

Both share: horizontal **day strip** with unfinished-day dots, **All / Day / 🌙 Night-watch** filter chips,
the **carried-over badge** (amber edge + "From Mon 2 Jun"), the **night-watch edge** (indigo), per-task
crew assignee + due, recurring badge, and FAB.

## What to Look For
- Does the day strip read instantly as "which day am I on"?
- Is the carried-over task obviously a roll-over, not a fresh job?
- Does Night Watch feel right as a **filter chip** (a kind of task) rather than a status tab?
- A vs B: is one-status-at-a-time calmer, or does seeing the whole day matter more on a boat?
